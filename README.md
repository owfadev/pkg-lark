# pkg-lark
## Why you'd want this

Your Feishu/Lark workspace becomes part of the workspace your agent already reads: docs, group files and calendar land as wiki pages, IM threads as archives.

- Stop shuttling between IM and your agent — ask in the workspace, get cited answers from your own docs
- Nothing leaves your account: pointer pages reference Lark, credentials stay local, the CLI is read-first
- Calendar events become timeline entries automatically; meeting follow-ups stop falling through cracks
